About Conway's Game of Life

Conway's Game of Life is a two-dimensional cellular automaton. Every cell is either alive or dead, and its eight neighbors determine its state in the next generation. A live cell survives with two or three neighbors; a dead cell is born with exactly three. These simple local rules produce oscillators, gliders, stable structures, and complex emergent behavior.

Click the grid to draw cells, use Ctrl + Click to place a glider, or Shift + Click to place a pulsar. The simulation engine runs in WebAssembly compiled from Rust.
